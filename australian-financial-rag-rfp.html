Executive Summary

Problem Statement

Gap 1: Interpretation & Application Challenge

Official financial information communicated through highly technical language, complex statistical releases, and policy documents designed for financial professionals. Residents lacking formal financial education struggle to understand how RBA decisions affect their financial situation.

Gap 2: Contextualization & Relevance Problem

Existing financial planning tools operate in isolation from evolving macroeconomic environment. Generic advice doesn't adapt to real-time conditions like inflation, interest rates, or government initiatives, leading to outdated or counterproductive guidance.

Solution Overview

RAG-based system integrating Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA), Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA), and Australian Securities Exchange (ASX) data into unified conversational interface.

  • Translates technical financial information into clear, actionable guidance
  • Dynamically integrates macroeconomic trends and policy changes
  • Provides personalized recommendations based on user profiles
  • Grounds all responses in authoritative sources with 98% citation accuracy

Lessons Learned & Future Enhancements

What Worked Well

  • Weekly coordination ensured alignment and rapid issue resolution
  • RAG architecture effectively grounded responses in authoritative sources
  • Phased approach allowed iterative refinement and early feedback
  • Multi-source integration provided comprehensive financial context

Future Enhancements

  • Expand to Mistral 7B for improved multi-turn dialogue capability
  • Implement proactive alerting for policy changes and opportunities
  • Conduct longitudinal studies tracking actual financial outcomes
  • Expand evaluation dataset for greater statistical confidence
