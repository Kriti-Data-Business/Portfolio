Recommended actions for next campaign wave
Scale — Email + In-store
Email (13.0x ROAS, +17.5%) and In-store Display (9.9x ROAS, +19.9%) both exceed the scale threshold. Recommend increasing Email budget by 40% and In-store by 25% for the next wave. Email's efficiency advantage makes it the priority allocation.
Optimise — Digital + Loyalty App
Digital Display and Loyalty App both sit at ~10% A/B lift with ROAS of 6–7x. Recommend A/B creative testing before scaling. For Digital, test category-specific creative. For Loyalty App, test offer value thresholds (currently 8% true uplift — room to improve).
