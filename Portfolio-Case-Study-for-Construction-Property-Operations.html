Problem Statement

Large construction and property firms operate across multiple interconnected business domains.

1
Data Fragmentation
▼

The Problem

Customer information, project data, financial records, and compliance documentation originate from different systems.

Real-World Impact

  • Duplicate customer records (CUS002 appears twice with variations)
  • Inconsistent address formats (postcode mismatches)
  • Invalid data formats (emily.davis.com missing @)
  • Missing critical fields (contract date, phone number, postcode)

Business Consequences

Duplicate records trigger duplicate contracts. Wrong postcodes delay building permits. Missing finance data blocks approvals. Cascading errors create expensive rework.

2
Administrative Overhead
▼

The Problem

Manual data entry and task management consume 2-3 hours per staff member per week on verification and status reporting.

Real-World Impact

  • Staff manually checking 50+ records (30-45 minutes per session)
  • Status reports generated by email chain requests
  • Task tracking scattered across multiple tools/emails
  • Duplicate work when tasks reassigned or escalated

Business Consequences

2-3 hours per person per week × team size = significant cost. Staff frustrated by repetitive work. Higher turnover. Lower morale. Mistakes increase as fatigue sets in.

3
Compliance & Regulatory Risk
▼

The Problem

Building permits, finance approvals, and contracts require data integrity and auditability.

Real-World Impact

  • No audit trail showing who changed what and when
  • Missing inspection records create compliance gaps
  • Contract errors (wrong data) lead to legal disputes
  • Finance approval chains break with incomplete customer data

Business Consequences

Regulatory fines. Delayed permits costing thousands per day. Legal exposure. Reputational damage. Loss of customer trust. In construction, compliance failures can be fatal.

4
Operational Visibility Gaps
▼

The Problem

Leadership and project managers lack real-time visibility into data health and project status.

Real-World Impact

  • "Is our data quality improving?" - No dashboard, no answer
  • "Which projects are delayed?" - Must manually check each project
  • "How many customers have incomplete records?" - Manual count required
  • "Which subcontractors are reliable?" - Tribal knowledge only

Business Consequences

Decisions are reactive, not proactive. Issues go unnoticed until they escalate. Strategic planning impossible without data visibility. Competitive disadvantage.

5
Subcontractor Coordination
▼

The Problem

Tracking task assignments, completion status, and delays across hundreds of subcontractors is complex and error-prone.

Real-World Impact

  • Site Masters has only 33% on-time completion rate (1 of 3 projects on-time)
  • Delays in one subcontractor cascade to dependent phases
  • Project handover dates slip due to untracked delays
  • Communication gaps lead to duplicate work or missed tasks

Business Consequences

Project cost overruns (delays = cost). Customer dissatisfaction. Penalty clauses triggered. Damaged reputation in market. Lost future business.

Solution Architecture

Three interconnected modules designed to address all five problem areas holistically.

1
Data Quality Engine
▼

Purpose

Automated detection and flagging of data anomalies before they impact downstream systems.

How It Works

  • Email Validation: REGEXMATCH(email, "^[\w\.-]+@[\w\.-]+\.\w+$") catches emily.davis.com as INVALID
  • Phone Format: LEN(phone)=10 validates Australian 10-digit standard
  • Postcode Check: MATCH(postcode, PostcodeList) against official list
  • Duplicate Detection: COUNTIF(ID:ID, ID)>1 identifies CUS002 appearing twice
  • Missing Fields: IF(field="", "MISSING", "OK") flags blank data
  • Quality Score: 100 - errors_weighted = final score (0-100)

Results

Baseline: 7 of 10 records flagged. Action flags: APPROVE (≥90) = 3 records, CHECK (75-89) = 4 records, REVIEW (<75) = 3 records.

2
Workflow Automation Tool
▼

Purpose

Streamline task management with automatic alerts and communication templates.

How It Works

  • Task Tracking: Status dropdown: Pending → In Progress → Completed, with automatic date logging
  • SLA Timer: TODAY() - DueDate = Days Late. Formula: IF(TODAY()>DueDate, "OVERDUE", "ON TRACK")
  • Auto Alerts: IF(Days Late > 0, "🚨 X DAYS LATE", "✓")
  • Priority Levels: CRITICAL → HIGH → MEDIUM → LOW with color-coding
  • Communication Template: Auto-generate email: "Task [ID] due [Date] - Status [Status] - Action Required"

Results

Real Results: TASK004 3 days overdue (Subcontractor RFQ) auto-flags CRITICAL. TASK007 5 days overdue (Payment Schedule) triggers escalation. Communication auto-generated with details.

Data Validation Engine - Deep Dive

Technical implementation of validation logic with formulas and real results.

Validation Methods & Formulas

1
Email Validation (REGEXMATCH)
▼

Formula

=IF(REGEXMATCH(B2,"^[\w\.-]+@[\w\.-]+\.\w+$"),"VALID","INVALID")

What It Does

Checks email format using regex pattern: [text]@[domain].[extension]

Real Examples from Dataset

  • john.smith@gmail.com → VALID ✓
  • emily.davis.com → INVALID (missing @) ✗
  • jessica.lee@ → INVALID (missing domain) ✗

Why It Matters

Invalid emails break communication and downstream system integrations. Catching these early prevents customer communication failures.

2
Phone Format Validation (LEN)
▼

Formula

=IF(LEN(C2)=10,"VALID",IF(C2="","MISSING","INVALID"))

What It Does

Validates Australian phone format: exactly 10 digits (0412345678)

Real Examples from Dataset

  • 0412345678 → VALID ✓ (10 digits)
  • 04123456 → INVALID (8 digits, too short) ✗
  • [blank] → MISSING (no data) ✗

Why It Matters

Phone format inconsistency breaks SMS alerts, calling systems, and automation. Standardization is essential for operational efficiency.

3
Postcode Verification (MATCH)
▼

Formula

=IF(ISNUMBER(MATCH(E2,Postcodes!A:A,0)),"VALID","INVALID")

What It Does

Cross-references postcode against official Australian Postal Code reference list

Real Examples from Dataset

  • 3141 → VALID ✓ (Oakleigh, Victoria)
  • 3150 → VALID ✓ (Moorabbin, Victoria)
  • [blank] → INVALID (missing postcode) ✗

Why It Matters

Wrong postcodes cause building permit delays, failed mail delivery, incorrect site allocation. Government systems depend on accurate postcodes.

4
Duplicate Detection (COUNTIF)
▼

Formula

=IF(COUNTIF($A$2:$A$999,A2)>1,"DUPLICATE","OK")

What It Does

Counts how many times each customer ID appears. More than 1 = duplicate record.

Real Examples from Dataset

  • CUS001 appears 1 time → OK ✓
  • CUS002 appears 2 times → DUPLICATE ✗ (once with sarahjohn@yahoo.com, once with s.johnson@yahoo.com)

Why It Matters

Duplicate customer records trigger duplicate contracts, duplicate billing, split payment tracking, and regulatory confusion. One duplicate costs hours of rework.

5
Quality Score (Weighted Formula)
▼

Formula

Base: 100 points Deductions: InvalidEmail(-15) + InvalidPhone(-15) + MissingPhone(-10) + InvalidPostcode(-15) + MissingPostcode(-10) + MissingFields(-8 each) + Duplicate(-20)

What It Does

Calculates comprehensive quality metric (0-100) with severity weighting. Duplicates worst (-20), missing fields lighter (-8).

Real Examples from Dataset

  • CUS001 (john.smith): 100 - 0 = 100 → APPROVE ✓
  • CUS002 (sarah): 100 - 10 (missing phone) = 90 → APPROVE ✓
  • CUS004 (emily): 100 - 15 (invalid email) - 16 (missing data) = 69 → REVIEW ✗
  • CUS002 duplicate: 100 - 20 (duplicate) = 80 → CHECK (borderline)

Why It Matters

Single metric enables prioritization: REVIEW records first (highest errors), then CHECK, then APPROVE clean records. Efficient use of limited resources.

How Validation Caught Errors
▼

Invalid Email (CUS004)

Value: emily.davis.com | Formula Result: INVALID (missing @ symbol)

How System Caught It: REGEXMATCH pattern ^[\w\.-]+@[\w\.-]+\.\w+$ requires @ character. emily.davis.com doesn't match. Flagged automatically.

Missing Phone (CUS002)

Value: [blank] | Formula Result: MISSING

How System Caught It: IF(C2="","MISSING","OK") checks for blank cells. Critical field is empty. Flagged for review.

Duplicate Record (CUS002)

Values: Two entries with CUS002 ID (different emails but same customer)

How System Caught It: COUNTIF($A$2:$A$999,A2)>1 counts CUS002 occurrences. Count = 2, so DUPLICATE flag. Prevents duplicate contracts.

Phone Too Short (CUS007)

Value: 04123456 (8 digits) | Formula Result: INVALID

How System Caught It: LEN(C7)=10 checks exact length. Length = 8. Doesn't match Australian standard. Flagged for correction.

PRJ-CUS001 (John Smith) - Delay Cascade Example
▼

The Project

StageExpectedActualDelay
Pre-Construction Oct 31 Nov 5 +5 days
Sitework Nov 30 Dec 5 +5 days (cumulative: +10)
Framing Dec 31 Dec 8 (in progress) +8 days projected

How Delay Cascade Works

Stage 1 Delay: Pre-Construction finishes 5 days late (Nov 5 vs Oct 31). Doesn't impact Sitework directly if Sitework can start immediately.

Stage 2 Delay: Sitework starts Nov 6 (day after Pre-Const). Expected to finish Nov 30, but finishes Dec 5 (5 more days). Cumulative = 5+5 = 10 days behind schedule.

Stage 3 Impact: Framing depends on Sitework completion. Can't start until Dec 5 instead of Nov 30. Loses 5 days. If Framing takes 30 days, now finishes Jan 4 instead of Dec 30. 5-day cascade continues.

Real Business Impact

Customer Impact: John Smith promised handover Jan 31. Now looking at Feb 5-10 handover. 1+ week customer disappointment.

Financial Impact: Each week delay = site costs (insurance, security, etc.), labor inefficiency, equipment rental.

Operational Impact: Subcontractor chain blocked. Elite Framing can't start. Their crew allocated elsewhere. Ripple effect across portfolio.

Formula: Actual Date - Expected Date = Days Late
IF(Days Late > 0, "AT RISK - " & Days Late & " days late", "ON TRACK")

Dashboard Shows: PRJ-CUS001 at 10 cumulative days delay = HIGH RISK. Manager sees this and takes action: accelerate Framing, add crew, adjust downstream projects.

Excel/Sheets Functions Used

Core Functions & Their Purpose
▼

COUNTIF - Error Counting & Duplicate Detection

=COUNTIF(range, criteria) → Counts how many cells match criteria

Used For: Count duplicate records, aggregate error types, calculate error statistics

XLOOKUP / INDEX-MATCH - Cross-Field Data Lookup

=XLOOKUP(lookup_value, lookup_array, return_array) → Modern alternative to VLOOKUP

Used For: Pull subcontractor info for task, fetch postcode validation from reference list

REGEXMATCH - Pattern Validation

=REGEXMATCH(text, regex_pattern) → Matches text against regex

Used For: Email format validation, complex pattern matching

IF / Nested IF - Conditional Logic

=IF(condition, value_if_true, value_if_false) → Branch logic

Used For: All validation decisions, error flagging, status determination

LEN - String Length Check

=LEN(text) → Returns number of characters in text

Used For: Phone format validation (must be exactly 10 characters)

DATEDIF / TODAY - Date Calculations

=DATEDIF(start_date, end_date, "D") → Days between dates

Used For: Calculate task overdue days, delay tracking, SLA monitoring
